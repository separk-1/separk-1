**Ph.D. Candidate · Civil and Environmental Engineering · Carnegie Mellon University**<br>
Advised by Pingbo Tang.

I study how humans and AI make decisions together in engineering systems. My work combines knowledge graphs, large language models, and multimodal sensing to support nuclear operations, water system design, and construction safety.

[Website](https://sparkpark.net) · [Email](mailto:seongeup@andrew.cmu.edu) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=G1eiHDcAAAAJ) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/separk111/)

## 01 / Selected research

| Area | Work |
| :--- | :--- |
| Nuclear procedure digitization | Multimodal document layout detection and LLM-based extraction to structure operating procedures as knowledge graphs. Summer Research Intern, **Idaho National Laboratory**, Jun–Aug 2026. |
| Water system design | A **GraphRAG** assistant for refining PFAS treatment designs from process flow diagrams to piping and instrumentation diagrams. Jul 2025–present. |
| Remote microreactor operations | Integrated procedure and incident knowledge graphs into operator interfaces through a GraphRAG decision-support assistant. Jul 2024–Jun 2026. |
| Construction safety | Suspended-object detection with **HangCon**, GPT-based accident prediction using **15,000 records**, and multimodal equipment activity recognition. |
