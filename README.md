## Research

I am especially interested in **design for operability**: turning complex engineering information into representations that people and AI systems can actually use during planning, monitoring, and abnormal situations.

Recent work includes:

- Building graph-structured representations of operating procedures and incidents for nuclear decision support.
- Developing GraphRAG assistants for procedure following, microreactor operation, and PFAS treatment design.
- Studying construction safety through object detection, activity recognition, and accident prediction.

## Background

Before joining Carnegie Mellon, I completed an M.S. in Architecture and Architectural Engineering at Seoul National University and a B.S. in Architectural Engineering at Kyung Hee University. I have also worked with Idaho National Laboratory on AI-supported nuclear procedure digitization.
