## Research

My current work focuses on:

- Graph-based operating procedures for nuclear decision support.
- GraphRAG assistants for engineering design and operation.
- Multimodal sensing and AI models for construction safety.

## Background

Before joining Carnegie Mellon, I completed an M.S. in Architecture and Architectural Engineering at Seoul National University and a B.S. in Architectural Engineering at Kyung Hee University. I have also worked with Idaho National Laboratory on AI-supported nuclear procedure digitization.
